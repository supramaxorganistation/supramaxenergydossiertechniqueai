### Dossier
- Definition：A project file representing a solar PV installation case, containing customer details, PV system parameters, equipment selections, computed compliance metrics, and attached documents. Each dossier moves through statuses DRAFT, PENDING_APPROVAL, APPROVED, REJECTED.
- Aliases：dossier、PV dossier

### STEG compliance
- Definition：The regulatory compliance check performed on a dossier to verify that the proposed solar PV installation meets Tunisian utility (STEG) requirements; the result is stored in complianceReport and rendered into a downloadable PDF annex.
- Aliases：STEG、compliance report

### ERP Modules
- Definition：The lightweight ERP subsystem built into the platform covering CRM (customers/suppliers), products, sales orders and invoices, purchase orders, inventory/warehouses, accounting (chart of accounts and journal entries), payments, HR (employees and attendance), and an aggregated dashboard — all mounted under the /erp route prefix.
- Aliases：ERP、erpRoutes
